## Task 4 — language fallback (i168): DONE (drafter)
FINDING: the brief's "MARKET_LANGUAGE map" does not exist. F22A.1(a) deleted the country map on 22 Sep (written languages
EN and DE only; order: their reply > Language field > EN). F24.4 adds a country map ONLY as the fallback when the Language
field is EMPTY, still clamped to EN/DE, and the reason is always recorded in draft_language_reason:
"company_country: Language field empty, inferred DE from the company's country (Germany)". A non-German country clamps to
EN with the reason naming it ("inferred FR … FR is not a written language, writing EN"). Switzerland is inferred as DE and
says "majority German (check for Romandie / Ticino)". No mappable country gives "default: no reply, no Language field
and no mappable company country (<value>), EN", so nothing is silent any more.
COUNT: live contacts with an empty Language field at a company whose country maps to a non-English language = **231**; of
those, **164** are at a German-speaking company (Germany, Austria, Switzerland) and now get DE (147 in Germany/Austria,
17 in Switzerland).
